# Editorial motion reference

## Dropbox Brand Guidelines

Source: <https://brand.dropbox.com/> (reviewed 2026-08-17)

The reference positions motion as one part of a consistent identity toolkit alongside voice, colour, imagery, typography, and iconography. Its page structure foregrounds a concise brand idea and lets visitors enter focused, single-subject guideline areas rather than presenting a dense visual collage.

Its dedicated motion guidance identifies four applicable principles: **prioritize simplicity**, **deepen understanding**, **provide immediate feedback**, and use **subtle playfulness** rather than overwhelming spectacle. It also publishes a decisive ease-in-out curve (`cubic-bezier(0.65, 0, 0.45, 1)`) and frames the goal as satisfying, purposeful movement rather than decoration.

## Visanam translation

Visanam will not copy Dropbox visuals, assets, or interface patterns. The applicable principles are: keep motion tied to a specific brand system; let one primary idea lead each viewport; choreograph transitions as a sequence rather than adding arbitrary effects; and make every interaction support orientation. For Visanam, this becomes a sunrise-to-night narrative with restrained depth, scene-linked section transitions, kinetic but legible editorial text, and gentle motion that always respects reduced-motion preferences.

For this iteration, the chosen motion language is: low-frequency parallax for atmosphere; staggered editorial entrances for orientation; a single scroll progress cue to make the journey understandable; tactile button feedback; and no continuous motion that competes with reading or story imagery.
