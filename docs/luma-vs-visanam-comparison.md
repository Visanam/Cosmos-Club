# Luma v003 Compared with Visanam

## Executive conclusion

The Luma reference is more explicit about its **parent conversion logic**. It makes a prospective buyer travel through a visible sequence: an emotionally familiar friction, a concrete explanation of the product, a child-versus-parent distinction, a trust commitment, and a low-pressure next action. Visanam is already stronger at **atmosphere, restraint, and world-building**. Its Oru film, day-to-night pace, compact navigation, and values language feel less like a conventional product page and more like an original family ritual.

The recommended direction is therefore **not to redesign Visanam in Luma’s image**. Instead, Visanam should keep its quiet cinematic identity and borrow only the reference’s operational clarity: show how the experience works, make trust specific, and make the parent outcome tangible before asking for a commitment. The result would feel more prominent because a parent can understand its value at a glance without losing the wonder of Oru.

## What is genuinely better in the reference

The reference is most effective where it removes ambiguity. It names the parent’s real need without blame, then connects that need to a concrete mechanism. The phrase structure is consistently legible: **what may feel hard → what the story makes possible → what the parent receives**. Its child and adult content are not simply described; they are visibly separated. That prevents a parent from asking, “Is this only a comic, or is this work for me too?”

The reference also makes trust part of the brand story instead of placing it only in a legal area. Its privacy, non-diagnosis, and claim-boundary messages are specific. This is valuable for Visanam because the current Parent Insight Journey is reassuring but could make its limits and data stance even clearer. The useful lesson is not its wording; it is the decision to answer practical parent questions before a visitor has to search for them.

## What Visanam should not give up

Visanam should not become a denser catalogue page. The current Home experience is more memorable because it begins in a place rather than with a checklist. The Oru film and the original abstract chapters establish a recognizable visual world, while the Values page already gives each principle a concrete child practice and familiar family moment. Those are meaningful competitive distinctions.

The reference also relies on a larger quantity of illustration panels and a direct “companion demo.” Visanam should stay more restrained. Reusing the same scenery repeatedly or putting every supplied illustration into a product grid would weaken the art direction that you have deliberately established. Each managed scene or film should have one editorial job: **arrival, threshold, practice, or return**.

## Page-by-page application

The **Home page** should remain a quiet cinematic entry. The immediate change is a single dual-audience line and a short three-step mechanism after the first scene. This will give the user the same clarity that the reference achieves while preserving Visanam’s less commercial, more atmospheric opening.

The **Enter Oru page** should act as Visanam’s distinctive proof of craft. The reference uses static illustrated scenery inside a scroll journey. Visanam can be more memorable by making its existing video intentional: add a usable poster image, a replay affordance, a precise eight-second label, and carefully timed text that does not compete with the film.

The **Values page** should stay simplified. Removing story glimpses was the right decision. The improvement is not more narrative content; it is a small, consistent `what this looks like at home` line and a path to begin the Parent Journey. This preserves the page’s clarity and avoids rebuilding the removed story shelf in another form.

The **Why Visanam page** is the best place to add operational proof. Its current relationship, story, and journey framing is already strong. Adding the four-part trust strip there will ensure that claims, age guidance, privacy expectations, and non-diagnostic boundaries are visible before a parent enters a more personal flow.

The **Parent Journey** is already more complete than the reference’s modal because it produces a paced plan. The key opportunity is to make the fourth step feel like an earned result rather than just the last screen of a form. A single richly designed plan preview, with a clear `you can adjust this any time` note, will improve perceived value without collecting more information.

## Originality and experience guardrails

The proposed work intentionally excludes the reference’s brand language, `Story Compass` concept, card layouts, Warli-inspired line band, image crops, font combination, written claims, questionnaire wording, and six-episode product architecture. Visanam should use its own Oru film, supplied scenery, Eczar and Manrope typography, lantern and orbit language, and existing value model.

The visual rule is straightforward: use scenery sparingly, avoid repetition on adjacent pages, and reserve each cinematic asset for one meaningful narrative shift. The interaction rule is equally important: no child scoring, no diagnostic framing, no simulated urgency, and no collection of personal details unless the product need and consent boundary are plainly stated.

## Recommended first implementation package

The most effective first package is **Priorities 1–3**: the Home three-step clarity band, the Why Visanam trust strip, and the final-step Parent Journey preview. Together, these changes would make the value proposition easier to understand, more trustworthy, and more tangible while keeping the current Oru world intact. The Oru film controls and the pricing disclosure should follow as a second, focused pass.
