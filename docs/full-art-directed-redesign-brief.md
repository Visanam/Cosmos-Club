# Visanam: Art-Directed Redesign Brief

## Core creative position

Visanam should feel like an **evening story ritual**, not an educational product catalogue. Its visual language will be original: warm mineral dawn, indigo dusk, paper texture, quiet constellations, hand-drawn paths, layered foliage silhouettes, and small points of lantern light. These cues echo the emotional atmosphere of the supplied references—sunrise, sunset, village warmth, and magical discovery—without replicating their composition, characters, or illustration style.

The experience speaks to two audiences in one intentional sequence. A child first feels invitation and wonder; a parent then receives clarity, credibility, and a modest next step. This follows the strongest cross-site benchmark finding: child delight is paired with adult reassurance, while specific context and transparent process lower the cost of acting.[1][2][3]

## The parent journey

| Moment | Parent thought | Designed response | Primary action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Recognition | “This happens in our home.” | Lead with a familiar emotional moment, not a product claim. | Choose a moment |
| Reassurance | “This feels gentle and appropriate.” | Show the story ritual, age-aware language, and calm scope. | See how it works |
| Trust | “I understand what this will give us.” | Explain the repeatable method: story, question, small practice. | Explore a value |
| Imagination | “My child might actually return to this.” | Introduce the cinematic story world progressively. | Watch the story world unfold |
| Commitment | “I can begin without getting it perfect.” | Offer one confident, low-pressure parent-plan action. | Begin our first story |

## Home-page chapter system

The home page will be rebuilt as five deliberately paced chapters. The opening starts with sunrise and an uncluttered promise. The middle uses a short atmospheric motion sequence made from supplied scenery as an editorial interlude rather than decorative repetition. The next chapters explain the ritual, values, and parent benefit. The ending becomes a calm night-time invitation—not an aggressive conversion block.

| Chapter | Visual mood | Job in the narrative | Interaction treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dawn | Sand, apricot, pale sky | Make the parent feel seen. | One primary action; a secondary quiet link. |
| First path | Hand-drawn topography and paper | Explain how a familiar moment becomes a story. | Scroll-reveal steps; no cluttered card grid. |
| Story passage | Scenic cinematic sequence | Build a child’s curiosity and show the world’s scale. | Slow crossfades, shallow parallax, optional pause control. |
| Constellation | Deep teal and starlight | Make values feel discoverable rather than instructional. | Curated value paths and hover/tap details. |
| Lantern return | Midnight blue and warm gold | Turn the journey into a simple family ritual. | One strong primary action; one reassurance link. |

## Interaction and conversion rules

The design will use one high-emphasis action per viewport. Arrows imply a next chapter, while buttons announce a meaningful commitment; they will not compete. Hover and focus states will feel tactile through a small raise, underline draw, glow, or icon shift. Motion will be secondary to reading: it will reinforce hierarchy, use compositor-friendly transforms and opacity, and stop or simplify under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

The navigation should remain deliberately short: **Why Visanam**, **Values**, **Stories**, and a single parent-journey action. Supporting information belongs in the footer or contextual routes. This mirrors the benchmark pattern of separating immersive play from parent proof and structured choice.[4][5]

## Page-specific direction

The **Values** page should read as a constellation map: each value is a discovered waypoint with a story glimpse, familiar moment, and small practice. The **Why Visanam** page should feel like shelter—a doorway, an open book, and an honest explanation of what stories can and cannot do. The **Parent Journey** should feel like a guided chapter with visible progress, never an intake form. The **Stories** route should protect anticipation: characters appear only when a child has chosen to enter a chapter.

## Success measures

The redesign is successful when a parent can describe Visanam in one sentence, find the first action without scanning, understand why the experience is appropriate, and feel that the child is being invited rather than instructed. A child should sense a world worth returning to before they are asked to understand its lessons.
